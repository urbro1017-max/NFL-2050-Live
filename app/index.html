WHAT THIS BUILD GUARANTEES

Every first-team player from the current official Detroit and Buffalo depth charts is embedded in the app, so the starter page does not depend on a roster API. Every starter has a prior-performance line. Skill players and defenders use Week 1 individual stats where the official team stat pages surface them; offensive linemen use team blocking context because standard NFL box scores do not assign individual rushing/pass-protection totals. Live data is additive and cannot erase the verified starter dataset.
