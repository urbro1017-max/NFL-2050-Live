# GRIDIRON ATLAS 15.0 — GM LAB

Built on ATLAS 14.3 Production Hardening.

## ATLAS 15 headline
GM Lab turns the existing league + roster data into a front-office workspace without inventing contract or draft information.

### New GM Lab
- Dedicated GM Lab navigation and front-office visual identity.
- 32-team selector with persistent team choice.
- GM Command Center with roster size, verified starters, position-room health and decision flags.
- Roster Board grouped into QB/RB/WR/TE/OL/DL/EDGE/LB/CB/S/ST rooms.
- Position Rooms scored from verified depth-chart coverage and experience.
- Needs Board ranks internal roster review priorities using transparent roster-derived signals.
- Decision Desk generates a personnel review queue from the weakest roster rooms.
- Player cards jump directly into the existing Player Universe profile workflow.
- Cap/Contracts and Draft Capital are explicitly source-gated until reliable feeds exist. No fake salary, guarantees, contract years or draft picks are generated.

- Cross-workspace selected-game persistence.
- PostgreSQL archive and existing football intelligence systems.
